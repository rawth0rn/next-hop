Silicon Labs [announced](https://iot-now.com/2026/08/06/157784-silicon-labs-unveils-bg2b-its-lowest-power-bluetooth-le-soc-with-power-efficiency-security-and-integration/) the BG2B, a Bluetooth Low Energy (LE) System on Chip (SoC) with initial production hardware planned for 2027.

The BG2B uses a dual-output DC-to-DC power architecture and multi-core design to reduce power consumption across active, receive, and sleep modes. It delivers 14% to 15% lower microcontroller unit (MCU) active current and 1.1 µA Energy Mode 2 (EM2) sleep current with RAM retention compared to the company's previous lowest-power Bluetooth LE device. The SoC supports advanced Bluetooth Channel Sounding for secure ranging, including Mode 3, Normalized Attack Detector Metric (NADM), and Inline Phase Correction Term (Inline PCT). It meets Apple and Google Channel Sounding specifications for interoperability across mobile ecosystems. Silicon Labs provides a royalty-free ranging library with the development kit.

Integrated peripherals include Controller Area Network with Flexible Data-rate (CAN-FD) for wireless diagnostics in commercial vehicles and industrial equipment without separate bridge devices. The chip also integrates LED boost and four-channel LED sink drivers, which eliminates external driver circuitry for electronic shelf labels and smart retail devices. Additional features include dual 12-bit Analog-to-Digital Converters (ADCs) for simultaneous analog sampling and Variable Resistive Load (VRL) for battery health estimation. These integrated functions reduce component count.

For edge deployments, the lower active and sleep currents extend operational battery life for wireless sensors, asset tags, smart locks, and wearables. The CAN-FD integration allows direct wireless monitoring of vehicle fleets and industrial gear, cutting Bill of Materials (BOM) cost and Printed Circuit Board (PCB) area by removing the need for external Bluetooth bridges. Secure Vault technology provides hardware-backed security that supports Platform Security Architecture (PSA) Level 3 compliance, helping manufacturers meet regulations such as the EU Cyber Resilience Act. The Custom Part Manufacturing Service enables secure provisioning of unique device identities during production.

BG2B SoCs are available now through an early customer engagement program. Production modules are planned for 2027.
