Orange has joined the [quantum-safe In-Factory Profile Provisioning (IFPP) program](https://iot-now.com/2026/11/05/159230-acceleronix-idemia-orange-single-sku-quantum-safe-ifpp/) that Acceleronix and IDEMIA Secure Transactions launched at MWC Barcelona in February, enabling single stock-keeping unit (SKU) manufacturing for Internet of Things (IoT) devices that connect to the Orange network.

In-Factory Profile Provisioning moves cellular connectivity setup from the design phase to the final assembly line. Manufacturers load the operator profile onto the embedded SIM (eSIM) at the end of production, so devices leave the facility tested, provisioned, and ready to connect from first power-on. The process works in air-gapped manufacturing environments and supports all eSIM form factors, including the 2 mm by 2 mm MFF4 package that is roughly 87 percent smaller than the widely used MFF2. Quantum-safe binding protects operator credentials throughout the production process.

The program follows the GSM Association (GSMA) SGP.41 architecture and runs on existing SGP.32 eSIM IoT infrastructure. IDEMIA Secure Transactions provides the IFPP-ready eSIM with quantum-safe profile binding and secure delivery. Acceleronix supplies the IFPP Loader and factory orchestration tools that integrate with existing production lines and provide per-device traceability. Orange contributes the operator profiles and managed global connectivity. Original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) can deploy the solution today and upgrade to the forthcoming SGP.42 specification once it publishes.

For supply chain and operations teams, the model eliminates the need to manage multiple SIM variants and regional SKUs. Manufacturers build one hardware platform and assign connectivity order by order on the line, reducing inventory complexity and accelerating entry into new markets. The approach particularly suits compact, power-constrained devices such as asset trackers and sensors, where limited battery capacity makes in-field profile downloads problematic. By provisioning at the factory, teams avoid the logistics of coordinating late-stage connectivity configuration in the field.

Acceleronix and IDEMIA Secure Transactions will demonstrate the solution live at electronica 2026 in Munich from 10 to 13 November, and trial kits are available for OEMs evaluating factory provisioning with Orange profiles.
