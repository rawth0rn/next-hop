Quectel [shipped the EG216Q-GL](https://iot-now.com/2026/11/11/159316-quectel-eg216q-gl-bg950q-gl-cost-efficient-lower-power-upgrade/), a Long Term Evolution (LTE) Cat 1bis cellular module that maintains pin-to-pin compatibility with the vendor's existing EG2-footprint hardware. The module targets device makers currently using the EC2x series, EC200A series, EG25-G, EG25-GL, EG21-G, EG21-GL and UC200A-GL, allowing them to migrate to a more cost-efficient and lower-power platform without redesigning printed circuit boards or discarding existing development work.

The EG216Q-GL delivers up to 10 Mbps downlink and 5 Mbps uplink over a single main antenna, eliminating the need for multiple antennas in space-constrained device designs. It incorporates built-in Wi-Fi scanning to support location awareness without relying exclusively on Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) receivers, which reduces component count and power consumption for tracking applications. This suits commercial telematics, payment terminals, asset tracking, remote monitoring and control systems, smart safety equipment, and smart metering and grid infrastructure.

For practitioners managing deployed fleets, the module offers a way to address rising component costs while preserving capital invested in existing hardware layouts. The lower power profile extends device battery life and reduces thermal load, cutting operational expenses for deployments that require continuous fleet visibility or reliable card transaction processing in locations where Wi-Fi coverage is unavailable. Remote diagnostics capabilities supported by the module reduce the frequency of physical site visits, lowering maintenance overhead for distributed installations and remote assets.

Industry research identifies Cat 1bis as the fastest-growing cellular IoT technology by volume, with forecasts indicating it will reach roughly 43 percent of module shipments by 2030. Moving to this platform now places devices on a trajectory aligned with that growth while avoiding the expense and time of complete hardware redesigns. Quectel backs the module with reference designs, evaluation tools, technical support and antenna options to minimize integration time.
