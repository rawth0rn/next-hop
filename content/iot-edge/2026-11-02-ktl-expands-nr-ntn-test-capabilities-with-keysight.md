Korea Testing Laboratory (KTL) has selected Keysight’s New Radio Non-Terrestrial Network (NR-NTN) test solutions to expand its satellite communications validation capabilities, according to [Keysight and KTL advance NR-NTN validation for satellite connectivity](https://iot-now.com/2026/10/27/159032-keysight-ktl-nr-ntn-validation-satellite-connectivity/).

The deployment adds NR-NTN RF and Radio Resource Management (RRM) capabilities to KTL’s existing wireless validation portfolio. Keysight’s Wireless Network Emulation Solutions enable the lab to conduct pre-conformance and certification testing for satellite-enabled devices. This supports the industry’s transition from Long-Term Evolution (LTE)-based direct-to-device services toward NR-NTN, which accommodates broader device categories and higher-performance use cases than previous satellite connectivity standards. The shift to NR-NTN represents a move toward standardized 5G satellite integration rather than proprietary satellite links.

The setup addresses a necessary step in the certification pipeline. Device manufacturers, chipset vendors, network operators, and satellite service providers require standards-based validation to confirm performance and interoperability before commercial deployment. Without accredited testing facilities, device makers face uncertainty in meeting regulatory and operator requirements for satellite connectivity. Recent Global Certification Forum (GCF) approvals have expanded Keysight’s validated NR-NTN test case coverage across RF and RRM parameters, giving laboratories a defined path to certify devices under Third Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) standards.

For operators and equipment makers, the expanded testing infrastructure reduces deployment risk by providing trusted validation tools earlier in the development cycle. KTL’s Song Tae-seung noted that the infrastructure extends mobile communication testing capabilities accumulated from 2G to 5G technology into the satellite communication sector. This allows manufacturers to prepare for NR-NTN certification with standardized test procedures rather than ad-hoc validation methods. The capability accelerates time to market for satellite-enabled Internet of Things (IoT) devices and handsets by ensuring that hardware meets conformance requirements before submission to certification bodies.
