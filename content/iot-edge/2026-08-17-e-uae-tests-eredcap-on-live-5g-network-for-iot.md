e& UAE became the first telecom operator globally to announce the [successful testing of enhanced Reduced Capability (eRedCap) on its live network](https://iot-now.com/2026/08/17/157935-e-uae-leads-industry-transition-to-5g-native-iot-with-successful-testing-of-reduced-capability-readiness-on-a-live-network/), delivering throughput speeds of up to 10 Mbps while utilising a 5 MHz narrow bandwidth of NR-FDD spectrum. The demonstration achieved 5-10 year battery life for IoT devices, comparable to Low-Power Wide-Area (LPWA) technologies and improving upon RedCap's typical 1-3 year lifespan.

The test integrated a Data Transmission Unit (DTU) into the live commercial network to verify eRedCap services. By reducing peak data rates and narrowing channel bandwidth compared to standard 5G, eRedCap cuts device complexity and power consumption. This provides a native 5G Standalone (5G-SA) replacement for legacy LTE Cat-1 and Cat-1bis devices, matching them in cost, coverage, and battery life while positioning deployments for the 5G-Advanced ecosystem.

The technology targets massive IoT deployments including smart utility meters, industrial sensors and controllers, asset and fleet tracking, payment terminals, building-management systems, security devices, and selected wearables. By operating on 5G-SA infrastructure, eRedCap lets enterprises consolidate IoT traffic onto a single network architecture rather than maintaining parallel LTE and 5G paths. This reduces network and device fragmentation, simplifies deployment, lowers complexity, and improves scalability across IoT ecosystems. It also strengthens security and service assurance while simplifying device lifecycle management.

For network operators and enterprise IT teams, the live test demonstrates a commercially viable migration path as LTE networks approach eventual sunset. It supports long-term spectrum refarming by enabling IoT devices to run on narrow 5G channels without requiring full-capability 5G hardware. According to Abdulrahman Al Humaidan, senior vice president of access network development at e& UAE, the test gives utilities, manufacturers, logistics providers, and smart-city operators a credible path to scale connected devices and prepare for the industry's transition beyond LTE. The move also creates a more sustainable environment with massive IoT connectivity and extended battery life.
