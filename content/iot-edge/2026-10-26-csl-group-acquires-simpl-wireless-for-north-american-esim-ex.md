CSL Group [acquired](https://iot-now.com/2026/10/19/158940-csl-group-acquires-simpl-wireless-critical-connectivity-north-america/) SIMPL Wireless, a US-based provider of Internet of Things (IoT) embedded SIM (eSIM) connectivity, giving the UK-based critical connectivity firm a North American operations base and expanding its multi-carrier eSIM capabilities.

SIMPL Wireless operates EverSIM, a multi-carrier eSIM built on unmodified Thales embedded universal integrated circuit card (eUICC) infrastructure compliant with GSMA SGP.22 and SGP.32 specifications. The technology supports 5G Standalone and remains backwards-compatible with narrowband Internet of Things (NB-IoT). A single SIM stock keeping unit carries a native primary carrier profile, with the ability to localize via over-the-air profile downloads. Backup profiles activate automatically when primary networks fail, returning control when service restores. Customers can bring their own connectivity from up to 100 mobile network operators or purchase through SIMPL while retaining full control of every connection and contract.

The company also offers Compass, a single-pane-of-glass management platform providing one dashboard and application programming interface to activate, monitor, and manage SIMPL SIMs alongside existing carrier SIMs, including automated self-healing through auto-profile fallback. This unified approach allows enterprises to manage every SIM and contract from one place, lowering costs and accelerating deployment timelines that historically stretched to a year or more down to days.

For practitioners managing cross-border IoT estates, the acquisition addresses fragmentation in SIM lifecycle management. SIMPL's platform allows enterprises to maintain existing SIMs, access point names, hardware, and carrier contracts while adding multi-network resilience without custom development or new platform integrations. The combination with CSL's patented rSIM technology and IoTM Solutions' connectivity orchestration, acquired in July 2026, creates a unified operating model for managing both traditional SIMs and eSIMs through one system with automated failover for critical applications.

The deal brings immediate Tier-1 carrier relationships, including T-Mobile, and an established US customer base across retail, transport, energy, and electric vehicle charging sectors. This gives CSL local go-to-market capability in North America alongside its existing more than three million connections in building security, healthcare, and utilities. The SIMPL team will join CSL Group, with SIMPL continuing to serve existing customers as a CSL Group company.

Financial terms were not disclosed.
