Space42 and Viasat [signed a binding agreement](https://iot-now.com/2026/09/15/158411-space42-viasat-equatys-direct-to-device-satellite-connectivity/) to establish Equatys, a $1 billion joint venture that will operate a shared satellite and ground infrastructure platform for direct-to-device connectivity.

The venture adopts a Tower Company model. This proven terrestrial mobile approach lets operators share standardized infrastructure instead of building duplicate networks. Equatys will provide neutral, shared satellites and ground systems. Multiple mobile operators can maintain their own spectrum licenses, customers, and commercial relationships while using common hardware. The initial constellation scales to 2,800 satellites across 60 orbital planes and three altitude layers. This design enables capacity densification without redesign as demand grows. The architecture supports high levels of spectrum aggregation, coordination, and cooperation among multiple licensees.

Space42 holds coordinated L-band spectrum rights in more than 160 markets. Viasat brings L-band spectrum globally and S-band in Europe. Together they have commercial agreements with over 400 mobile operators worldwide. They also have access to more than 100 MHz of globally coordinated Mobile Satellite Service (MSS) spectrum for direct-to-device and advanced MSS services. The platform builds on 3GPP Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) standards. These standards enable everyday smartphones and IoT devices to connect without specialized hardware. Additional spectrum licensees and satellite operators can use the constellation without being required to invest in the system.

For network operators and IoT practitioners, Equatys converts satellite capacity from a proprietary vertical into shared infrastructure. Operators can extend coverage into gaps, including dense urban areas where terrestrial signals falter, without launching their own constellations. The shared model reduces capital requirements and accelerates deployment while preserving commercial independence. The addressable market is projected to reach $30 to $70 billion by 2040. This growth will be driven by 3GPP Release 17 and later standards that embed satellite access directly into consumer devices. The business model is open by design, allowing ecosystem participants to reduce capital requirements and increase service availability compared to individual constellations.

Upon formation, each founder will commit $400 million in initial equity. Space42 will add another $200 million in a future funding round open to third-party investors. Viasat is expected to serve as prime technology contractor. The formation remains subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions.
