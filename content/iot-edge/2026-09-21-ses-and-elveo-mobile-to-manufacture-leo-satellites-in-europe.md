SES and Elveo Mobile have expanded their collaboration to manufacture Low-Earth Orbit (LEO) direct-to-device (D2D) satellites in Europe, using [Elveo’s patented technology](https://iot-now.com/2026/09/16/158437-ses-elveo-mobile-direct-to-device-services-europe/).

The companies will establish manufacturing capabilities and a supply chain in Europe for producing the LEO D2D satellites. These spacecraft will use Elveo’s patented satellite bus, payload, and software stack. The LEO constellation will form part of a multi-orbit architecture that combines Elveo’s LEO D2D network with SES’s existing geostationary (GEO) and Medium Earth Orbit (MEO) networks. This integration aims to deliver secure and reliable connectivity across the combined orbital layers for European users.

The expanded collaboration targets mobile roaming and connectivity for European consumers, businesses, governments, and institutional users, including the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO). The service uses Elveo’s high-priority 2 GHz S-band spectrum rights, which currently cover more than one billion people. SES plans to distribute these D2D services globally while enabling the roaming capabilities for European mobile operators and government agencies that require secure communications outside terrestrial coverage.

For network operators and systems architects, the arrangement creates a regional manufacturing base for LEO D2D infrastructure within European territory. This addresses supply chain and data sovereignty requirements often raised by government and defence customers. The multi-orbit design offers operational redundancy by combining the low latency characteristics of LEO with the coverage stability of GEO and MEO networks. Operating in the 2 GHz S-band allows direct connectivity to standard mobile devices without requiring specialized satellite terminals. This simplifies integration for service providers and reduces friction for end users who need connectivity in remote or underserved areas across the continent. The partnership focuses on accelerating delivery timelines for European customers, as emphasized by the chief executives of both companies.
