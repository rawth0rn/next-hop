The Global Certification Forum (GCF) and the Mobile Satellite Services Association (MSSA) have [signed a Memorandum of Understanding](https://iot-now.com/2026/09/28/158580-gcf-mssa-memorandum-of-understanding-ntn-certification-interoperability/) to formalize certification and interoperability testing for 3rd Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) devices.

The agreement covers direct-to-device (D2D) services, NTN 5G NR, and NTN NB-IoT (Narrowband Internet of Things). Under the arrangement, the organizations will develop certification requirements and testing frameworks using GCF's existing certification infrastructure. GCF has offered conformance testing for NTN NB-IoT in geostationary orbit (GEO) satellite systems since 2024, with ongoing work to expand coverage to non-geostationary orbit (NGSO) constellations, including low Earth orbit (LEO) systems, and to include NTN 5G NR technology. The collaboration will also promote interoperability and roaming between terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks, enabling mobility between terrestrial and satellite coverage.

The collaboration addresses fragmentation in satellite IoT deployment. Without unified certification, devices certified for one satellite constellation might fail to roam or interoperate with others, forcing enterprises to manage multiple hardware variants for global coverage. This creates supply chain complexity and limits economies of scale. The MoU establishes a scalable certification model that validates devices against 3GPP standards rather than proprietary operator requirements. MSSA will coordinate access to its member operators' networks for field testing in operational conditions, moving validation beyond lab environments and ensuring devices perform under actual satellite link conditions.

For network operators and enterprise IT teams, this reduces the risk of stranded assets and procurement complexity. A single certification mark will indicate that a device works across both terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks and across different NTN constellations. This aligns with GCF's existing work with 3GPP and the GSM Association (GSMA), creating a consistent path from lab conformance to live network operation. The framework supports personal communication, Internet of Things (IoT), mission-critical, and automotive applications.
