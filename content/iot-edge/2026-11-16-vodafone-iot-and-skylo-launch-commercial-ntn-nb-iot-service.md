Vodafone IoT and Skylo have [launched](https://iot-now.com/2026/11/10/159299-vodafone-iot-skylo-launch-commercial-satellite-connectivity-service/) a commercial satellite connectivity service that allows enterprise devices to switch between cellular and satellite networks using a single standard SIM. The service uses Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) Narrowband IoT (NB-IoT) technology to extend coverage beyond terrestrial limits without requiring hardware changes or separate subscriptions.

The integration connects Vodafone IoT's core network directly with Skylo's satellite infrastructure. Devices roam between terrestrial and satellite networks automatically, with sessions managed through Vodafone IoT's Managed Connectivity Platform. This platform already orchestrates over 240 million connections, and now applies the same policy controls, billing, and monitoring to satellite links as it does to cellular ones.

Skylo built its network on Third Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) standards, which allows unmodified NB-IoT devices to connect using existing Vodafone global SIM cards. The approach eliminates the need for bespoke satellite hardware and separate vendor management contracts that typically complicate hybrid deployments. Coverage extends to remote regions where terrestrial infrastructure is unavailable.

Early trials ran in the UK, Spain, Italy, and Canada with applications including remote storage tank monitoring and micro mobility tracking. The commercial launch targets sectors such as asset tracking, logistics, maritime, agriculture, mining, and remote energy infrastructure.

For network operators and enterprise IT teams, the significance is operational consolidation. Satellite connectivity becomes a standard roaming option rather than a separate network silo requiring different management tools. Customers provision and monitor all connections through one platform and one SIM, reducing complexity for deployments that cross coverage boundaries regularly. The unified management removes the need to coordinate between cellular and satellite vendors when troubleshooting connectivity issues.

The service is available to new and existing Vodafone IoT customers immediately. It addresses coverage blackspots without requiring enterprises to negotiate separate satellite contracts or maintain multiple SIM inventories for different geographic regions.
