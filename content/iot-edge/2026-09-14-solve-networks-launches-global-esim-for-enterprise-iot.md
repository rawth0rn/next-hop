Solve Networks launched global eSIM connectivity for enterprise IoT deployments, giving customers a single profile that works across more than 200 countries and territories [source](https://iot-now.com/2026/09/10/158338-solve-networks-global-esim-connectivity-enterprise-iot/).

The service targets organizations managing distributed device fleets that cross borders. Instead of juggling separate SIMs, carrier relationships, and points of failure, customers get one connectivity profile that moves with their devices. Solve Networks manages this through its existing Clarity™ portal, the same interface customers already use for domestic multi-network SIM deployments. This portal integration means existing customers can add global coverage without learning a new management system.

Coverage spans North and South America, Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. The eSIM connects across networks in each region without requiring local arrangements or physical SIM swaps. A single profile follows devices as they move, eliminating the need to provision different SIMs for different markets.

Solve Networks built the offering on its experience in industrial and oil & gas connectivity, where downtime is a liability rather than an inconvenience. The company maintains a near-100% customer retention rate in those sectors. That standard for reliability and hands-on support now extends to broader enterprise use cases, including manufacturing, logistics, fleet, and energy operations with international or multi-region footprints.

For network operators, the value is operational simplicity. One profile means fewer vendor relationships to manage, less logistical overhead for SIM provisioning, and consistent security policies across borders. Stephen Mammen, VP of engineering at Solve Networks, noted the approach challenges common assumptions about solving connectivity problems. The service is available now to new and existing customers.
