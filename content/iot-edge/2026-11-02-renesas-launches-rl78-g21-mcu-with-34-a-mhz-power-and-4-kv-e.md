Renesas Electronics announced the RL78/G21, a new 16-bit MCU targeting smart appliance system control, expanding its RL78 family with lower power consumption and enhanced security capabilities [IoT Now](https://iot-now.com/2026/10/29/159101-renesas-rl78-g21-ultra-low-power-16-bit-mcu-smart-appliances/).

The RL78/G21 achieves an operating current of 34 μA/MHz, which Renesas reports is nearly half the consumption of the existing RL78/G13 devices. This performance allows designers to reduce battery capacity and downsize power supply circuits. The device adds a UARTA communication interface for sensor expansion and includes a high-speed on-chip oscillator with ±1.0% accuracy. It integrates 4KB of data flash memory, removing the need for an external EEPROM. The MCU covers 128KB and 256KB code flash options and operates across a voltage range from 1.6V to 5.5V.

Pin and function compatibility with the RL78/G13 and RL78/G23 lets practitioners migrate existing designs with minimal redesign. The new parts start at US$0.56 for the 128KB version and US$0.77 for the 256KB version in the 44-pin LQFP package. Renesas markets the chip at appliance makers for equipment including air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, and microwave ovens. The company also pairs the MCU with other components to form solution packages such as a Smart Remote-Controlled Tankless Gas Water Heater and a Household Water Pump.

The RL78/G21 offers ±4 kV human body model electrostatic discharge tolerance and IEC 60730-compliant self-diagnostic functions to support manufacturing reliability and safety compliance. Security features include flash read protection, a shield window, and unique identifiers to guard against cloning and unauthorized access. Renesas supports development with the Smart Configurator tool and the Fast Prototyping Board. The RL78/G21 devices are available today.
