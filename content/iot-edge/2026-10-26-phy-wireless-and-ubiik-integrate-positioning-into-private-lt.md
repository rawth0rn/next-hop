PHY Wireless and Ubiik have [announced](https://iot-now.com/2026/10/21/158947-phy-wireless-ubiik-3gpp-cellular-positioning-physical-ai-industrial/) an integrated communications and positioning platform for private Long Term Evolution (LTE) and 5G networks. The offering merges Ubiik's private LTE and 5G infrastructure with PHY Wireless' hellaPHY edge positioning technology to provide meter-level location accuracy for industrial and enterprise environments.

The platform calculates location directly on the device rather than in the cloud. This edge-based approach lets devices decide when they need location updates, which reduces network traffic, bandwidth use, and power draw. The companies state that the architecture can lower device power consumption by up to 10x compared with alternative approaches. Because it uses standard 3rd Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) cellular signaling, the system runs over existing private LTE and 5G deployments and does not require separate proprietary positioning networks.

Operators can consolidate two functions onto one infrastructure. Instead of maintaining distinct radios for communication and location, enterprises can use a single private cellular network for both voice, data, and asset tracking. The result is lower deployment cost and simpler operations, particularly in warehouses, manufacturing floors, and other complex indoor environments where traditional Global Positioning System (GPS) and Wi-Fi location systems often fail. The platform targets asset tracking, workforce safety, inventory management, and logistics, and the integration is intended to support emerging Physical AI and sensing workloads that rely on accurate device location.

The immediate benefit for practitioners is the ability to add meter-level positioning to existing private cellular networks without new cabling or specialized beacons. That makes the upgrade path quieter and easier to budget than rolling out a parallel location system.
