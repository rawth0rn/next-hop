CesiumAstro [raised $730 million in Series D financing](https://www.satellitetoday.com/finance/2026/11/06/cesiumastro-raises-730m-series-d-to-fund-synchronicity-constellation/) to build its Synchronicity satellite constellation, a 737-satellite low Earth orbit system that will use active phased arrays for dynamically managed multi-beam communications across Ku-, Ka-, and V-band frequencies.

The Austin-based company designed the silicon, active phased array, payload, processors, spacecraft, ground terminals, and network as one vertically integrated system. CEO Shey Sabripour stated this approach allows the company to build capabilities that evolve as mission requirements and operating conditions change. The constellation features an AI-enabled architecture with advanced edge compute and multi-orbit operations. CesiumAstro launched its first satellite, Element-1, on an October Transporter rideshare mission.

Synchronicity will operate in Non-Geostationary Orbit (NGSO) to provide fixed satellite service for enterprise, government, industrial, and mobility users. The company filed with the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) in July to establish the system, which uses electronic beam steering without mechanical movement to redirect capacity between targets.

For network operators, the funding creates a new vertically integrated supplier that owns its semiconductor fabrication, mission software, and terminal design. This year CesiumAstro acquired Jariet Technologies, 1Aardvark, and Vidrovr to consolidate semiconductor, mission software, and artificial intelligence capabilities. The Series D round, led by Janus Henderson with participation from Insight Partners and Toyota fund Woven Capital, values the company at more than $4.5 billion. The company also secured $100 million in debt financing through the Export-Import Bank of the United States (EXIM) and JPMorgan.

CesiumAstro plans to expand U.S. manufacturing and spacecraft production with the new funding. That concentration of domestic production matters for practitioners managing sovereign or defense networks subject to requirements for domestic sourcing and supply chain security.
