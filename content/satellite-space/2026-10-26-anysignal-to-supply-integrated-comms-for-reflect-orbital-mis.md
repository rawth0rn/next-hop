[AnySignal](https://spacenews.com/anysignal-to-deliver-space-to-earth-communications-for-three-reflect-orbital-missions/) will supply space-to-earth communications for three Reflect Orbital missions, providing both flight radios and ground network software. The agreement covers the USX space transceiver for onboard operations and the AnyLink platform for ground connectivity.

The USX unit is a multi-band software-defined radio that consolidates high-rate payload downlinks and Telemetry, Tracking, and Command (TT&C) functions within a single hardware module. By combining these capabilities, the design removes the need for separate radios dedicated to specific frequency bands. This integration reduces mass and cabling requirements on the satellite bus, freeing up volume and power budgets for the primary mission payload. The consolidation also simplifies thermal management by reducing the number of heat-generating components. The software-defined architecture permits operators to reconfigure modulation schemes and frequency bands in flight through software updates rather than hardware swaps, providing flexibility as regulatory or operational requirements change.

For ground operations, AnyLink functions as an abstraction layer that aggregates access to multiple ground-station-as-a-service (GSaaS) providers. Rather than building proprietary ground stations or negotiating individual contracts with disparate networks, operators access third-party antennas through a single software interface. This model shifts capital expenditure to operational expenditure and provides geographic diversity for contact windows without maintaining owned infrastructure. The unified approach also simplifies operations by presenting a single application programming interface for scheduling contacts across different ground station networks.

Reflect Orbital is developing spacecraft that redirect sunlight to precise Earth locations to extend solar power generation and support nighttime construction or search-and-rescue operations. For this mission profile, maintaining continuous command links and downlinking payload telemetry are operational necessities, but RF engineering is not a core competency. By adopting AnySignal's integrated stack, Reflect Orbital delegates the radio hardware design and ground network orchestration to a specialized vendor. This allows the engineering team to focus resources on the spacecraft's mirror systems, attitude control, and orbital mechanics rather than communications infrastructure.

The partnership covers Reflect Orbital's inaugural satellite and two subsequent missions. It illustrates a shift toward commoditized space communications, where software-defined radios and aggregated ground networks reduce the engineering overhead for specialized satellite operators and accelerate deployment timelines for novel space applications.
