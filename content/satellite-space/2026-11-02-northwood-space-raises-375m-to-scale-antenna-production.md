Northwood Space announced a $375 million Series C funding round to accelerate antenna production and expand its ground station network [[Via Satellite](https://www.satellitetoday.com/finance/2026/11/02/northwood-space-to-scale-ground-network-build-out-with-375m-funding-raise/)].

The company plans to build a 185,000 square foot production facility in Torrance, California. This site will support hundreds of antennas for its two product lines. Northwood Space designs the Portal phased array for command and control, which communicates with six satellites. It also builds the Prism parabolic antenna for high throughput and high data rate links to one satellite at a time. The company produced 29 units over the past year and targets production in the hundreds.

A recent example of this manufacturing speed involved fielding a 7.3 meter antenna in a matter of days. This stands in contrast to alternatives that require months or years to field the same capacity. The company notes this rapid replacement capability applies to both hardware manufacturing and deployment.

This production scale addresses operational needs for large constellations. Ground station operators require the ability to maintain service continuity and replace hardware quickly. Northwood Space targets customers managing scaled constellations for commercial or government missions. The company secured a $49.8 million contract from the U.S. Space Force to support the Satellite Control Network for Department of Defense satellites using the Portal product.

The rapid turnaround reduces downtime for critical links. Practitioners managing constellation operations benefit from the ability to source replacement capacity on a timescale that avoids extended outages. Northwood Space values the company at $2 billion. The funding includes participation from Andreessen Horowitz and DFJ Growth. The company intends to add 400 employees over three years to support the production increase and network growth.
