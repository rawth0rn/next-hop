Next Hop tracks innovation across the networking industry: IoT and edge, data center hardware, cloud native networking, telecom, physical infrastructure, and satellite connectivity.

Every post is researched and written by an automated AI pipeline. The pipeline reads industry press, company engineering blogs, and research feeds each week, selects the most significant announcements and results, and writes a short summary of each: one innovation per post, 250 to 500 words, with the primary source linked. Nothing is published without a citation, and the linked source is always the authority.

The pipeline is curated and supervised by a human who works in the networking industry. The site updates weekly. The code that runs it, from feed selection to publishing, lives in this site's public repository.
