Marvell agreed to acquire Lumentum for $80 billion in stock, assuming debts that bring the enterprise value to $115 billion, in a move to secure supply of indium phosphide lasers for AI interconnects. [The Next Platform](https://www.nextplatform.com/connect/2026/11/03/marvell-cuts-to-the-front-of-the-datacenter-optics-line-with-lumentum-deal/5307392)

Lumentum is one of two primary suppliers of the indium phosphide lasers required for optical interconnects that bypass the physical limits of copper signaling in AI clusters. The deal gives Marvell control over a critical component for near-package optics (NPO) and co-packaged optics (CPO), technologies needed to connect GPUs, XPUs (custom accelerators), and memory within racks and across rows at 800G to 1.6T speeds. Marvell already builds custom CPUs and XPUs for Amazon Web Services, Microsoft, and Google. This acquisition lets it offer a vertically integrated stack from compute silicon through optical modules, covering scale-up networks inside racks and scale-in networks linking components on a package or tray. The combination addresses the so-called copper wall where electrical signaling hits bandwidth limits at the rack and package level, requiring photonics for scale-up and scale-in fabrics that need roughly ten times the bandwidth of the layer above them.

For network operators, the acquisition addresses a concrete supply constraint. According to Marvell chief executive officer Matt Murphy, the order book for photonics lasers is already sold out through early 2029. By owning Lumentum, Marvell can prioritize laser supply for its own custom chip customers while still selling components to Nvidia, AMD, and others. This secures a path for Marvell to compete with Broadcom in the custom silicon market without facing the same procurement bottlenecks that currently constrain high-bandwidth memory availability. The deal follows Marvell's earlier acquisitions of Inphi and Celestial AI, which added PHY technology and photonics interposer capabilities for optical on-package compute engines. Marvell expects the scale of the Lumentum business to be an order of magnitude larger than its Inphi acquisition was at the time of purchase.
