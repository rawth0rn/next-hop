Virginia's governor signed an executive order restricting data center development, acknowledging that datacenters have become a "political cancer." The order limits permitting, bans certain non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), and mandates stronger environmental protections, according to coverage in [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/21/amazon-shows-metas-muse-ai-shopping-agent-the-door/5297777).

The executive order represents a major policy shift for Virginia, one of the largest data center markets in the United States. The state has attracted major cloud providers through tax incentives and available land, but rapid growth has created political backlash. The order specifically puts "the kibosh on NDAs," which have historically shielded project details from public scrutiny, and imposes stricter environmental requirements on new facilities.

For network operators and infrastructure teams, the permitting limits will directly impact deployment timelines. Projects that previously moved through streamlined approval processes now face additional hurdles and public disclosure requirements. The NDA ban means companies can no longer keep facility locations, power consumption figures, or water usage data confidential during the approval phase. This transparency could complicate negotiations for land and power contracts, as competitors and community groups gain earlier access to project details.

The environmental protections add another layer of complexity. Operators must now budget for more extensive impact studies and potentially costly mitigation measures for water and energy consumption. The policy change signals that Virginia may no longer offer the permissive regulatory environment that made it attractive for massive data center builds. Companies planning multi-year capacity expansions will need to factor in these new constraints or consider alternative regions with more predictable regulatory frameworks. The immediate effect of the order leaves no grace period for projects already in the pipeline.
