Cisco is [partnering with Supermicro](https://www.networkworld.com/article/4213820/cisco-bulks-up-its-ai-infrastructure-portfolio-with-supermicros-liquid-cooled-servers.html) to bring liquid- and air-cooled high-density servers into its Secure AI Factory with Nvidia architecture, directly addressing the power and cooling challenges of modern AI data centers. The companies announced the expansion will begin in October, adding Supermicro’s rack-scale and dense GPU systems to the validated Cisco AI infrastructure portfolio.

The partnership enables rack-to-fabric liquid cooling, where Cisco’s liquid-cooled N9000 Series Switches interoperate directly with Supermicro’s liquid-cooled compute nodes. This creates a unified thermal management system from server to network fabric. Cisco states that modern rack-scale systems like the Nvidia NVL72 can exceed 200 kW per rack, making liquid cooling a requirement rather than an option. The combined solution targets trillion-parameter training and high-throughput inference use cases using platforms including Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 and Nvidia HGX Rubin NVL8.

For network operators, the integration matters because it removes thermal and power constraints that previously limited such workloads to hyperscale operators. By using common operating models and existing technical competencies, organizations can deploy these systems without developing new proprietary skill sets. The solution also extends Cisco’s existing 100 percent liquid-cooled AI networking systems to cover the entire rack, engineering the fabric for performance efficiency alongside the compute it serves. This reduces operational complexity while enabling denser AI clusters.

Cisco will validate and sell the Supermicro systems as part of its broader Secure AI Factory portfolio, which includes Cisco UCS servers powered by Nvidia GPUs, Cisco Silicon One or Nvidia Spectrum-X switching, security tools such as Cisco AI Defense, and Splunk for monitoring. The packaged system can be validated end-to-end under Cisco and Nvidia architecture qualification programs to accelerate deployment and reduce risk.
