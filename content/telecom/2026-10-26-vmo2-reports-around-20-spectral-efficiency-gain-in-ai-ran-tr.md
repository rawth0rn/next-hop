[Virgin Media O2 (VMO2)](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/operators/vmo2-lauds-ai-impact-on-ran/) reported an around 20% improvement in spectral efficiency during a test of AI technology in the Radio Access Network (RAN) with Nokia at a busy UK transport hub, describing the trial as the first of its kind in the nation.

The trial deployed machine learning algorithms within the RAN to optimize radio resource allocation in real time at a high-traffic commercial location. Spectral efficiency measures how much data transmits per unit of spectrum, so gains translate directly to additional capacity without requiring new spectrum licenses or additional radio units. AI-based RAN optimization typically adjusts beamforming patterns, power levels, and scheduling decisions based on traffic predictions and instantaneous channel conditions. VMO2 and Nokia did not disclose the particular transport hub location, the frequency bands involved, or the AI model architecture used.

Transport hubs concentrate thousands of simultaneous users, creating persistent congestion that stresses traditional static radio configurations. An improvement of around 20% allows an existing cell site to serve more subscribers or deliver higher throughput during peak hours without physical infrastructure upgrades. For operators, this suggests a path to defer capital expenditure on new spectrum or hardware densification in dense urban corridors while maintaining service quality. These venues often see demand spikes that exceed average urban traffic by an order of magnitude, making them ideal test beds for validating capacity optimization techniques under realistic load.

The result adds to growing evidence that AI-driven RAN optimization can move from laboratory simulations to live network environments. If the gains hold across broader deployments, operators could extract more value from existing spectrum allocations before participating in expensive auction rounds. The trial also serves as a reference point for other UK operators considering similar vendor integrations.
